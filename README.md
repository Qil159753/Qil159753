### MIONEX — myoelectric prosthetic arm

Co-founder & CTO, MIONEX / IntelliHand LP.

A powered arm for below-elbow amputees. I designed the control board, the firmware, and the
single-channel surface EMG front end, and I fitted the arms to their users. Six arms delivered
under a completed government contract (TED Fund YP004/2566). The arm was covered on Thai PBS.

Shipping today: single-channel EMG, one reliable grip, 3D-scanned individual socket,
in-house control board, SLS PA12 shell at 0.65 kg. Multi-channel EMG and muscle-pattern
recognition are still in the lab.
